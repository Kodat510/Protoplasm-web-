Protoplasm

Protoplasm is a game that follows the charecter slimy, who was originally a human but got involved in a fatal accident.. his friend managed to save him using a technology protoplasmic transfusion. this caused him to become an anamorphic fragile slime, and was kept in containment by the government for experiments. he gets a chance to escape after he was freed from his cell due to a bomb during the war.
